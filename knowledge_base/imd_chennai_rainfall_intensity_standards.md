## 1. Official IMD Rainfall Intensity Categories
Precipitation recorded in a 24-hour period (08:30 IST to 08:30 IST) is categorized per standard IMD terminology:
- **No Rain / Trace:** 0.0 mm to 0.1 mm
- **Light Rain:** 0.1 mm to 15.5 mm
- **Moderate Rain:** 15.6 mm to 64.4 mm (requires stormwater drain monitoring; urban traffic slowdown)
- **Heavy Rain:** 64.5 mm to 115.5 mm (local ponding in low-lying corridors; subway waterlogging alerts active)
- **Very Heavy Rain:** 115.6 mm to 204.4 mm (widespread inundation risk; reservoir discharge monitoring mandatory)
- **Extremely Heavy Rain:** > 204.4 mm (severe catastrophic flood alert; disaster response mobilization)

## 2. Weather Alert Color Code System for Greater Chennai
- **Green (No Warning):** Normal weather; no operational advisories needed.
- **Yellow (Watch / Be Updated):** Moderate rain expected over 24-48 hours. Zonal officers verify pump readiness.
- **Orange (Alert / Be Prepared):** Heavy to very heavy rain (64.5 mm to 204.4 mm). Rapid Response Teams deployed to waterlogging chokepoints. Subways monitored.
- **Red (Warning / Take Action):** Extremely heavy rainfall (>204.4 mm) or severe cyclonic cloudburst. Full disaster emergency operations center activated. School and college closures recommended.

## 3. Key Chennai Observatories & Geographic Distribution
- **Nungambakkam (Zone IX - Central Urban):** Primary historical station since 1813. Mean annual rainfall ~1,380 mm.
- **Meenambakkam (Zone XII - Airport / South):** Aviation observatory; represents southern urban and airport basin.
- **Sholinganallur (Zone XV - South Coastal IT Corridor):** Marine-influenced rainfall; sensitive to squall lines.
- **Madhavaram & Puzhal (Zone III - North Retteri):** Northern catchment feeding Retteri and Red Hills lakes.
