## 1. Critical Arterial Corridors in Chennai
Chennai's road mobility depends on five major arterial spines:
1. **Anna Salai (Mount Road):** 16 km core arterial connecting Chennai Central to Guindy Kathipara Junction.
2. **Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR / IT Corridor):** 20 km southern corridor from Madhya Kailash to Siruseri.
3. **Grand Southern Trunk Road (GST Road):** Connects Kathipara, Chennai Airport, Tambaram, and southern districts.
4. **East Coast Road (ECR):** Coastal highway from Thiruvanmiyur through Kottivakkam to Kovalam.
5. **100ft Inner Ring Road (Jawaharlal Nehru Road):** Beltway connecting Koyambedu, Vadapalani, Ashok Nagar, and Kathipara.

## 2. Vulnerable Railway Subways & Automatic Closure Triggers
During severe downpours (>40 mm/hr or accumulation exceeding 30 cm depth), the following railway subways are closed to all vehicular traffic:
- **Gengu Reddy Subway (Egmore):** Divert via EVR Periyar Salai and Spurtank Road.
- **Madley Subway (T. Nagar):** Divert via Usman Road Flyover and Burkit Road.
- **RBI Subway (Parrys Corner):** Divert via Raja Annamalai Mandram and Wallajah Road.
- **Rangarajapuram Two-Wheeler Subway:** Divert via Bazullah Road and Arcot Road.
- **Vyasarpadi & Ganesapuram Subways:** Divert via Basin Bridge Flyover.

## 3. High-Density Traffic Classification Criteria
- **Low Congestion (Class 0):** Free-flow speeds > 40 km/h; total vehicle count < 50 per observation window.
- **Normal Flow (Class 1):** Minor delay; speeds 25-40 km/h; vehicle count 50-90.
- **High Congestion (Class 2):** Significant slowing; speeds 15-25 km/h; vehicle count 90-130.
- **Heavy / Gridlock (Class 3):** Creeping or stopped traffic; speeds < 15 km/h; vehicle count > 130. Trigger adaptive signal timing and traffic police field deployment.
