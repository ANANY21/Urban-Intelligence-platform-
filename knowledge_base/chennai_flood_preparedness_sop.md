## 1. Executive Summary & Administrative Zoning
Greater Chennai Corporation is divided into 15 administrative zones comprising 200 wards. During the Northeast Monsoon (October to December), Chennai receives approximately 60% of its annual rainfall (~850 mm). The coastal topography is predominantly flat, with average elevation ranging between 2 meters to 10 meters above Mean Sea Level (MSL), making stormwater drainage heavily reliant on tidal windows and macro-canals.

## 2. River Basin Command Structures & Threshold Warning Stages
Chennai's drainage network discharges across three primary river systems:
1. **Adyar River Basin (Zones IX, XII, XIII):**
   - Surplus discharge trigger from Chembarambakkam Reservoir: First warning at 5,000 cusecs; Second warning at 10,000 cusecs; Critical flood stage at >15,000 cusecs.
   - Vulnerable low-lying zones: Jafferkhanpet, Kotturpuram, Saidapet, Surya Nagar, and Chitra Nagar.
2. **Cooum River Basin (Zones V, VII, VIII, IX, X):**
   - Vulnerable bridge chokepoints: Aminjikarai, Koyambedu, Chetpet, Radhakrishnan Nagar.
   - Critical clearance height under bridges: 1.5 meters minimum freeboard.
3. **Kosasthalaiyar River Basin (Zones I, II, III):**
   - Poondi Reservoir surplus release to Ennore Creek.
   - Vulnerability: Manali industrial belt, Sadayankuppam, and Madhavaram lowlands.

## 3. High-Capacity Dewatering Pump Deployment Protocols
- **Pre-monsoon Stage:** GCC pre-deploys 980 heavy-duty diesel dewatering pump sets (capacity ranging from 50 HP to 100 HP) across 418 identified chronically waterlogged spots.
- **Micro-Tractor Pumps:** Mounted on 4WD tractors for narrow ward lanes in Velachery, Madipakkam, and Pulianthope.
- **Operational Rule:** Any street ponding exceeding 15 cm depth triggers immediate automated dispatch of the nearest mobile suction unit.

## 4. Ward-Level Evacuation & Relief Center Readiness
- **Designated Relief Centers:** 169 community relief centers pre-stocked with emergency dry rations, water purification kits, backup diesel generators, and 24/7 medical supplies.
- **Evacuation Trigger:** Water level exceeding 0.75 meters in residential areas or imminent Chembarambakkam discharge exceeding 12,000 cusecs mandates mandatory ward-level evacuation coordinated by GCC Disaster Teams and NDRF/TNDRF battalions.
- **Emergency Helpline:** GCC Crisis Control Room: 1913. TNSDMA Control: 1070.
