## 1. Network Overview & Macro-Drainage Architecture
The Greater Chennai stormwater drainage system comprises 3,080 km of roadside integrated concrete box drains, connected into 33 major natural macro-canals, including:
- **Buckingham Canal:** 48 km north-south conduit traversing Central, South, and North Chennai parallel to the Coromandel coast.
- **Otteri Nullah:** 10.8 km drainage canal conveying runoff from Anna Nagar, Kilpauk, and Purasawalkam to Buckingham Canal.
- **Captain Cotton Canal:** Serving Vyasarpadi, Perambur, and Kodungaiyur.
- **Mambalam Canal:** Carrying stormwater from T. Nagar, Kodambakkam, and West Mambalam into the Adyar River.
- **Veerangal Odai:** Crucial link carrying runoff from Nanganallur, Madipakkam, and Adambakkam into Pallikaranai Marshland.

## 2. Key Drainage Deficits and Hydraulic Constraints
1. **Low Hydraulic Gradient:** The coastal gradient in central Chennai is less than 1:1,000, reducing gravity discharge velocity during high-tide phases.
2. **Pallikaranai Marshland Shrinkage:** The original marshland area of ~5,000 hectares has shrunk to ~600 hectares due to historical urban encroachment, severely lowering south Chennai's natural buffer and retention storage.
3. **Macro-Canal Desilting Mandate:** Desilting of Buckingham Canal and Cooum surplus channels must be completed by September 30 annually to maintain minimum 80% hydraulic cross-sectional clearance.
4. **Tidal Lockout Effect:** During astronomical high tides in the Bay of Bengal (tides reaching +1.2m to +1.4m MSL), sea water pushes upstream through estuary mouths (Adyar, Ennore, Muttukadu), preventing gravity storm runoff and necessitating flap gates and high-lift pump stations.

## 3. High-Priority Low-Lying Vulnerability Clusters
- **Velachery & Madipakkam (Zone XIV):** Low-lying saucer topography adjoining Pallikaranai marshland; requires continuous twin 100-cusec dewatering pumping at AGS Colony and Ram Nagar.
- **Perumbakkam & Sholinganallur (Zone XV):** IT Corridor belt; vulnerable to marsh backflow when Okkiyam Maduvu bottleneck exceeds 12,000 cusecs.
- **Pulianthope & Vyasarpadi (Zone V/VI):** Depressed basin with limited natural runoff gradient; reliant on Stephenson Road pumping station.
- **Kolathur & Retteri (Zone VII):** Retteri surplus channel integration and Red Hills surplus canal monitoring.
